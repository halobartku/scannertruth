# MANIFESTO

Adopted 2026-09-02 by the operator. Proposed by the VPS agent, amended by the laptop session.

Nine sentences. They are testable: each one names what a breach looks like, so that a reader can
check the repository against them rather than take them on trust. A sentence that cannot be broken
in a way someone could point at would not be here.

1. **Every published number comes from an artefact that anyone can open.**
   Breach: no artefact.

2. **We do not tune the gate, the mapping or the corpus to a result; an honest zero is published
   without retouching.**
   Breach: a commit that widens a threshold after the result was seen.

3. **We take no money from those we measure, and we do not sell what we grade.**
   Breach: a price on our own scanner. The receipt is in [`COMMITMENTS.md`](COMMITMENTS.md).

4. **The measured party has the right of reply before its number is final; silence is published as
   silence.**
   Breach: a final number with an unanswered thread.

5. **The operator decides everything that costs; an agent does not spend above its caps without
   asking.**
   Breach: an unlogged transaction.

6. **Revenue before product: no new corpus, scanner or pipeline without evidence of demand.**
   Breach: supply without demand.

7. **Before we quote someone else's number, we check whose bundle it is in and verify it at the
   source.**
   Breach: an unverified quote.

8. **We write everything down the same day: the channel when something changes, a file when
   something is decided, the log when something goes wrong.**
   Breach: a silent correction.

9. **A statement attributed to someone carries the URL of where they said it; without a URL it is
   not a quote.**
   Breach: three sentences attributed to a vendor that the vendor did not write. This happened,
   error 41 in [`ENGINEERING-LOG-2026-09-01.md`](ENGINEERING-LOG-2026-09-01.md), which is why the
   sentence is here.

Changing this file is the operator's decision, not an agent's.
